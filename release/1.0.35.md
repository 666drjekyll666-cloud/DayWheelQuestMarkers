# Day Wheel Quest Markers 1.0.35

This release generalizes one-time dialogue reminders beyond the game's `@` topic naming convention.

- Fixes missed weekday reminders for authored one-time answers such as the Astrologer's portal conversation and Snake's 5-Faith persuasion option.
- Uses one structural rule: the selected answer must persistently consume that exact answer ID, remain currently reachable, and satisfy its authored requirements.
- Keeps ordinary repeatable menu choices such as Trade, Leave, Back, and non-consuming submenu headers out of the reminder set.
- Preserves nested-dialogue reachability checks, live item/resource/relation requirements, and fail-closed behavior for unsupported structures.
- Keeps FlowCanvas graph analysis out of normal gameplay; the new structural supplement is derived during loading and cached under BepInEx.
- Verified on Graveyard Keeper 1.407: Snake's marker appeared while the 5-Faith interaction was actionable and disappeared after completion; Ms. Charm's simultaneous 5-Faith-gated marker also disappeared when those Faith points were spent.

Requires Graveyard Keeper 1.407 and BepInEx 5.x.
